Radova Systems Engineering & Packaging Cohort

4-week cohort covering reproducible Linux packaging, systemd hardening, and archival storage on Radova.

When
07–28 Dec 2026 · 19:00–21:00 WIB (UTC+7)
Where
Online Cohort
Quota
20 seats

Overview

Build, sign, and present a production-ready Radova package repository over four weekly live sessions.

Syllabus

  1. Week 1 · Dec 07 Reproducible builds, clean chroots, and Radova package manifests.
  2. Week 2 · Dec 14 Systemd isolation, seccomp filters, and least-privilege daemons.
  3. Week 3 · Dec 21 Checksummed archival storage, snapshots, and recovery drills.
  4. Week 4 · Dec 28 Capstone defense: ship a signed Radova package repository.

Prerequisites

  • Fluent in Linux CLI, Git, and shell scripting.
  • Radova Linux 2026.11+ running locally or in a VM.
  • Available for four live Monday sessions (19:00–21:00 WIB / UTC+7).

Mentor

Radova Core Team · Maintainers of Radova packaging, Memora, and Coretez.
